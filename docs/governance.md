# Model governance

The platform is a prioritization aid. It does not determine legal compliance or certify supplier performance.

## Controls

- Every estimate records its method and quality tier.
- Uncertainty is returned with the point estimate.
- Environmental and human-rights signals remain visible as separate components.
- High-priority suppliers require human review and a documented remediation owner.
- Synthetic demo factors must be replaced with approved organizational factors before production use.

## Known limitations

Spend-based emissions are sensitive to price changes and sector averages. Country-level risk can hide local variation. Supplier self-reported evidence requires verification. Scores should be calibrated with procurement, sustainability, compliance and affected stakeholders.
